How to read it

  • Camelot wheel: keys next to each other (8A→9A) or straight across (8A→8B) share most notes and blend smoothly.
  • Energy boost/drop: two steps round the wheel lifts or lowers the mood on purpose.
  • Tempo: gaps over 6 BPM are tricky to beatmatch; over 10 BPM is a hard jump.

The full beginner guide is in theory.md.

What it can't do

It doesn't connect to Spotify or Apple Music, detect key or tempo from audio, or play music. Every key, BPM and energy value comes from the data you paste.
