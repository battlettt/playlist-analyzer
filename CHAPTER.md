# Music chapter

**Thesis:** Every great playlist is secretly a walk around a circle of twelve keys, so I built a tool that draws the map and shows you where the path stumbles.

**Project card: playlist-analyzer.** A zero-dependency Python CLI and offline browser demo that reads an exported playlist and shows which keys and tempos it lives in, how its energy rises and falls, and which back-to-back tracks blend or clash on the Camelot wheel. It scores the playlist's flow from 0 to 100 and suggests a better order that never drops a track and never scores worse. Along the way it teaches the theory in plain English: the circle of fifths, harmonic mixing, beatmatching and energy arcs.
